## With a small form

Keep popover forms to one or two fields; anything longer belongs in a [Dialog](/docs/components/dialog).

<Example title="Rename">
  <Popover>
    <Popover.Trigger asChild>
      <Button variant="outline">Rename</Button>
    </Popover.Trigger>
    <Popover.Content className="w-72">
      <div className="grid gap-2">
        <Input aria-label="Folder name" defaultValue="Lab results" />
        <Button size="sm" className="justify-self-end">Save</Button>
      </div>
    </Popover.Content>
  </Popover>
</Example>

`Popover.Trigger` takes `asChild` to use your own button.

## Accessibility

- The trigger gets `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`; focus moves into the content and returns to the trigger on close.
- Escape and outside clicks close it.

## RTL notes

- `align="start"` and `"end"` follow the reading direction, so they mirror in Arabic.
