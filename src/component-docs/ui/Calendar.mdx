## Look and language

- **Today** — hairline `border-strong` ring, never a fill.
- **Selected / range ends** — `bg-accent text-accent-fg`; the **range middle** sits on `bg-accent-subtle`.
- **Outside days** — `text-fg-subtle`; **disabled days** are struck through.
- Previous/next buttons are ghost icon buttons with translated labels and mirror in Arabic.
- The calendar's locale and direction follow the app language, and day numbers always use Western digits.
