import { RadioGroup } from '@components/ui';

## Stacked

Each item is the radio circle followed by its label, a plain inline choice rather than a field-like card. The whole row is the click target (at least 28px tall).

<Example title="Plan">
  <RadioGroup aria-label="Plan" defaultValue="clinic">
    <RadioGroup.Item value="solo">
      <RadioGroup.Indicator />
      Solo practice
    </RadioGroup.Item>
    <RadioGroup.Item value="clinic">
      <RadioGroup.Indicator />
      Clinic
    </RadioGroup.Item>
    <RadioGroup.Item value="hospital" disabled>
      <RadioGroup.Indicator />
      Hospital (contact sales)
    </RadioGroup.Item>
  </RadioGroup>
</Example>

## In a row

Short options read best side by side: lay the root out as a wrapping row.

<Example title="Inline">
  <RadioGroup aria-label="Visit type" defaultValue="in-person" className="flex flex-wrap gap-x-6 gap-y-2">
    <RadioGroup.Item value="in-person">
      <RadioGroup.Indicator />
      In person
    </RadioGroup.Item>
    <RadioGroup.Item value="video">
      <RadioGroup.Indicator />
      Video call
    </RadioGroup.Item>
  </RadioGroup>
</Example>

## Dot

`RadioGroup.Dot` is the same circle without behaviour, for custom single-select lists such as table filters.

<Example title="Non-interactive indicator">
  <RadioGroup.Dot checked />
  <RadioGroup.Dot />
</Example>

In forms, use [FormRadioGroup](/docs/components/form-radio-group).

## Accessibility

- Radix renders `role="radiogroup"` with `role="radio"` items; Arrow keys move and select, Tab leaves the group.
- Label the group with `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`; each item's text is its option name.

## RTL notes

- The circle sits at the inline start, before its label, so it moves to the right in Arabic; Arrow keys follow the reading direction.
