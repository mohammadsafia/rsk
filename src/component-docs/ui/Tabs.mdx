## Underline

The default: a 2px accent bar under the active tab.

<Example title="Underline">
  <Tabs defaultValue="overview" className="w-full max-w-md">
    <Tabs.List>
      <Tabs.Trigger value="overview">Overview</Tabs.Trigger>
      <Tabs.Trigger value="visits">Visits</Tabs.Trigger>
      <Tabs.Trigger value="billing">Billing</Tabs.Trigger>
    </Tabs.List>
    <Tabs.Content value="overview" className="text-fg-muted pt-3 text-sm">Patient summary and alerts.</Tabs.Content>
    <Tabs.Content value="visits" className="text-fg-muted pt-3 text-sm">Past and upcoming visits.</Tabs.Content>
    <Tabs.Content value="billing" className="text-fg-muted pt-3 text-sm">Invoices and payments.</Tabs.Content>
  </Tabs>
</Example>

## Contained

A sunken track with the active tab raised on the surface. Good for switching views of the same data.

<Example title="Contained">
  <Tabs defaultValue="day" className="w-full max-w-md">
    <Tabs.List variant="contained">
      <Tabs.Trigger value="day">Day</Tabs.Trigger>
      <Tabs.Trigger value="week">Week</Tabs.Trigger>
      <Tabs.Trigger value="month">Month</Tabs.Trigger>
    </Tabs.List>
    <Tabs.Content value="day" className="text-fg-muted pt-3 text-sm">Day view</Tabs.Content>
    <Tabs.Content value="week" className="text-fg-muted pt-3 text-sm">Week view</Tabs.Content>
    <Tabs.Content value="month" className="text-fg-muted pt-3 text-sm">Month view</Tabs.Content>
  </Tabs>
</Example>

## Accessibility

- Radix renders `tablist`, `tab` and `tabpanel` roles with `aria-selected` and `aria-controls`.
- Arrow keys move between tabs and activate them; Tab moves into the panel.

## RTL notes

- Tabs run right to left in Arabic, and Arrow keys follow the reading direction.
