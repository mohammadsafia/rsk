## Custom scale

`fullValue` sets what counts as full, so you can pass raw counts.

<Example title="3 of 8 steps">
  <div className="grid w-full max-w-sm gap-1.5">
    <div className="text-fg-muted flex justify-between text-xs">
      <span>Onboarding</span>
      <span dir="ltr" className="tabular-nums">3 / 8</span>
    </div>
    <Progress aria-label="Onboarding" value={3} fullValue={8} />
  </div>
</Example>

## Accessibility

- Radix renders `role="progressbar"` with `aria-valuenow` and `aria-valuemax`; give it an `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`.
- For unknown durations use a [Spinner](/docs/components/spinner) instead.

## RTL notes

- The bar fills from the inline start: left to right in English, right to left in Arabic.
