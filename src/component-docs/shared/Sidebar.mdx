## Expanded

Active items get the hover tint and `aria-current="page"`; there is never a coloured side stripe.

<Example title="Groups, badge and sub-menu">

    <Sidebar>
      <Sidebar.Header>
        <span className="text-fg text-sm font-semibold">Al-Noor Clinic</span>
      </Sidebar.Header>
      <Sidebar.Content>
        <Sidebar.Group label="Clinic">
          <Sidebar.Item href="/docs/components/sidebar" icon={CalendarDays} active>Schedule</Sidebar.Item>
          <Sidebar.Item href="/docs/components/sidebar" icon={Users} badge={4}>Patients</Sidebar.Item>
          <Sidebar.SubMenu icon={Receipt} label="Billing">
            <Sidebar.Item href="/docs/components/sidebar" icon={ClipboardList}>Invoices</Sidebar.Item>
          </Sidebar.SubMenu>
        </Sidebar.Group>
      </Sidebar.Content>
    </Sidebar>
  </div>
</Example>

## Collapsed

From `md` up, `collapsed` shows an icon rail; each item keeps its name and shows it as a tooltip.

<Example title="Icon rail">
  <div className="bg-sidebar border-border h-72 w-16 overflow-hidden rounded-lg border">
    <Sidebar collapsed>
      <Sidebar.Content>
        <Sidebar.Group label="Clinic">
          <Sidebar.Item href="/docs/components/sidebar" icon={CalendarDays} active>Schedule</Sidebar.Item>
          <Sidebar.Item href="/docs/components/sidebar" icon={Users} badge={4}>Patients</Sidebar.Item>
        </Sidebar.Group>
      </Sidebar.Content>
    </Sidebar>
  </div>
</Example>

Inside an [AppShell](/docs/components/app-shell), `collapsed` follows the shell's state and `Sidebar.CollapseButton` toggles it. `Sidebar.Content` scrolls when the menu is taller than the screen.

`Sidebar.Header`, `Sidebar.Footer` and `Sidebar.CollapseButton` (desktop only) complete the frame.

## Accessibility

- `Sidebar.Content` is a labelled `nav`; groups are `role="group"` with their label, and the current page has `aria-current="page"`.
- Collapsed items keep their accessible names; the tooltip repeats them for sighted users.

## RTL notes

- Icons sit at the start and badges at the end; the sub-menu chevron and the collapse button mirror in Arabic.

## In Next

Links go through the app's router (`KitLink`): in a Next app that is `next/link`, and the visitor's language goes in front of every path, so pass language-free paths (`/members`, not `/en/members`). Nothing to change between the two starters. The active item is read from the path without its language, so `/ar/members` highlights `/members`.
