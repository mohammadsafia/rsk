## Day stepper

You own the date and the step: the component calls `handlePrevious`, `handleNext`, and `onSetDate` with today's ISO date.

<Example title="Step by day" code={demoSource('DateNavigationDemo')}>
  <DateNavigationDemo />
</Example>

Step by week or month by changing what your handlers add. For a full calendar with this header built in, see [CalendarGrid](/docs/components/calendar-grid).

## Accessibility

- The arrow buttons have translated labels ("Previous", "Next"); the date is plain text next to them.

## RTL notes

- The arrows mirror in Arabic, so "previous" points right; the date is formatted for the app language with Western digits.
